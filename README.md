# Interactive-Tier-Lists

**[Ace Attorney Recurring Songs](https://pastebin.com/sKMuvak3)**  
Songs that are very similar to each other in how they're used in every game. For example, every game has a courtroom lobby, and thus a unique song that plays there.  

**[Ace Attorney Character Songs](https://pastebin.com/KS7wur2k)**  
Not every character has their own song, especially in the original trilogy, but there's still a ton of unique music that sticks with you.  

**[Ace Attorney Reminiscence Songs](https://pastebin.com/GfSs8AFq)**  
Any song in the soundtracks with "reminiscence" in its name can be found here. Antagonist confessions, tragic backstories, sad moments and such.  

**[Ace Attorney Location Songs](https://pastebin.com/qLNqqTe7)**  
Quite self-explanatory, but these are songs that you may associate with specific locations rather than characters, even though many will play for both.  

**[Ace Attorney "Folk" Songs](https://pastebin.com/Ukncc52S)**  
The smallest of the lists, "Folk" songs (not to be confused with traditional folk songs in real life) are used for multiple characters that embody a similar feeling.
